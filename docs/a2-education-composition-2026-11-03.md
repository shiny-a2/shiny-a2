# A2 Education: from enquiry to institute operations

Release 3.25.0 gives A2 Education an open introduction and a full-width scroll scene, aligned with the Watch and Sport presentations. Admissions, tuition and branch-management shortcuts open the relevant capability and move keyboard focus to it.

A dedicated section explains how A2 Learning covers the teaching experience and links to that product. Integration remains a separately agreed scope. The existing staging and acceptance wording is preserved; this presentation update does not establish production readiness for an institute. English product names remain readable within Persian paragraphs.

Local verification covered eight Chromium journey contexts and eight WebKit interactive/contrast contexts across Persian/English, mobile/desktop and light/dark themes. Watch journey regression, reduced-motion/no-JavaScript reading, content checks and 315 canonical URL checks passed. Representative introduction and learning-section screenshots were inspected. Physical-device performance and real institute operations were not tested.

Published at [A2 Education in Persian](https://amiraliyaghouti.com/fa/education-platform.html) and [English](https://amiraliyaghouti.com/education-platform.html). Live journey and interactive/contrast checks each passed all eight language/width/theme contexts, plus reduced-motion and no-JavaScript reading. All changed published files matched their release hashes.
