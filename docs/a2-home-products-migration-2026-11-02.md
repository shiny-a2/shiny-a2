# A2 homepage and product journey

The particle-cloud composition is now the design basis for the English and Persian homepage. It introduces A2's software and digital-marketing work, followed by service disclosures, selected product families, founder context and a contact route. The previous cinematic homepage moves to the product collection, where its reel, product gallery and connected-workflow sequence serve product discovery.

The searchable catalogue remains available from the collection. Its filters, bilingual search normalization, empty states and canonical product links are preserved. Continued scrolling moves from the homepage to products and then About. Existing contact anchors retain their destination with clearance below the header.

Reviewed titles, descriptions, entity/service evidence, canonical URLs and five-language alternatives remain intact. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript visitors receive readable content and links. The retained reference scene remains credited; it is not presented as original A2 artwork.

Local checks cover both languages, mobile and desktop, light and dark appearance, menu and disclosure interactions, scene reversal, catalogue discovery, end-scroll navigation and incoming anchors. Content and indexability checks pass for 315 canonical URLs; a repeat build reproduces 264 release files. Release 3.20.1 is published. Live checks pass Persian Chromium and English WebKit across both viewport sizes and themes, including the opaque reading-header regression. Both-language homepage-to-products-to-About transitions and contact anchors were verified live during the migration. Changed public files match release hashes. Physical-device performance and owner visual acceptance remain separate checks.

The next design stages are a distinct About page, a focused contact journey, an A2 Watch landing as the reusable pattern, and then the remaining product families. Those stages are not included in this migration.
