# A2 cinematic product landings — 3.4.0

The twelve A2 product families now have cinematic landing pages with a large product identity, a concise introduction, an immersive scroll scene, complete capability details and a visual next-product link. Each scene uses original conceptual artwork relevant to its business, from watch retail and gym membership to cafe ordering, learning and automation. Customers remain scoped delivery references rather than product names.

Native scrolling controls perspective, object motion and three workflow captions in either direction. A pause control freezes the scene without changing the page height. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript visitors receive a static illustration and the same complete text. The landing scenes use vectors and CSS depth, without loading the cinematic homepage's WebGL runtime or autoplaying video. The homepage still retains the separately credited reference 3D sequence; this release does not claim its replacement.

The six established industry landing URLs remain intact. Six additional product families and a complete catalogue now have canonical English and Persian routes. Titles, descriptions, social images, reciprocal language links, structured data, sitemaps and discovery guides use the shared A2 product content. Cinema copies remain noindex and point to the canonical landing. Homepage product links lead directly to these canonical pages.

Local validation passed 96 product contexts in Chromium and 96 in WebKit across all twelve products, both languages, mobile/desktop and light/dark appearance. Focused final checks cover reversible scrolling, pause without a layout jump, module/FAQ disclosure and reading fallbacks. Additional short-height checks caught and corrected caption/panel crowding. Content and link checks cover 24 canonical landings; sitemap checks pass for 315 canonical URLs. These checks do not establish search indexing, rankings or physical-device frame rates.
