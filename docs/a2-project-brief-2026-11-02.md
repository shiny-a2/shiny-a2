# A2 product-to-contact brief — 3.7.0

Visitors can now choose capabilities on any of the twelve bilingual A2 product landings and carry those choices into a reviewable project brief. For example, a watch retailer can select storefront and catalogue capabilities before describing the operational problem they want to solve. The existing direct-contact path remains available.

The contact page shows the selected capabilities, allows changes or clearing, and resets incompatible choices when a different product is selected. Switching between Persian and English carries the product and capability choices with their translated names. Typed free text is not carried across languages. Editing a field or selection clears the previous draft so it cannot be mistaken for an up-to-date request.

These are initial discussion priorities, not a placed order or a fixed delivery commitment. Only public product and capability identifiers enter the selection URL. Free-text requirements stay in the browser until the visitor chooses to copy the draft or open it in an email app. The form does not automatically send a message.

Local Chromium and WebKit checks pass eight language, viewport and appearance combinations per browser. They cover keyboard selection, transfer, translated choices, malformed and duplicate parameters, clearing, product changes, draft invalidation and no-JavaScript direct-contact fallback. Related family-page checks pass navigation, module disclosures and draft behavior. Static checks retain 315 canonical sitemap URLs. Light/dark, mobile/desktop selection and draft states were visually reviewed. Email delivery was not tested, and no conversion gain or owner acceptance is claimed.
