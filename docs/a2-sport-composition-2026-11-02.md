# A2 Sport: a clearer club-management journey

Release 3.24.1 brings A2 Sport into the shared product presentation: an open introduction, a full-width pinned scene and three business entry paths for membership, acquisition/renewal and club management. A separate specialist-services section links to coaching, massage booking and café ordering. Each shortcut opens the existing capability and moves keyboard focus to its summary.

The delivery wording reflects the reviewed public Sam Fit documentation: selected workflows have local candidate evidence, while operational and hardware acceptance remain incomplete. Specialist services retain separate development and acceptance scopes. Sam Fit remains a delivery reference, not the A2 product name.

Watch and Sport share the same presentation implementation and preserve their existing capability selections and contact briefs. Local checks passed eight Chromium journey contexts for each product and eight Sport WebKit interactive/contrast contexts, covering both languages, mobile/desktop widths and light/dark themes. Reduced-motion and no-JavaScript reading also passed. Content and canonical-URL checks pass. These checks concern the portfolio interface; they do not validate live club operations or physical-device performance.
