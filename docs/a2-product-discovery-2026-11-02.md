# A2 product discovery — 3.6.1

The A2 catalogue now helps visitors find an offering by product name, industry or capability, then narrow the results by business need. Search handles Persian/Arabic letter and digit variants and names typed without spaces. A result count, combined filters, an empty state and reset make selection clearer. Search terms stay in the browser and create neither requests nor query URLs. Without JavaScript, all twelve product links remain available.

Each landing also introduces three related A2 offerings. These describe adjacent business needs; their combined scope and integrations are defined during discovery. The catalogue introduction is shorter so the selection tools appear sooner on mobile.

Checks cover English/Persian, mobile/desktop and light/dark appearance in Chromium and WebKit, including combined filters, normalized searches, empty/reset states, current-product navigation, Escape and no-JavaScript/reduced-motion paths. The existing family regression passes modules, FAQs and local-only contact drafts. Group and related-product references are validated, and sitemap checks retain 315 canonical URLs. No ranking gain or owner visual acceptance is claimed.
